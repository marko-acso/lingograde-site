It is a professional assessment tool, not an accredited exam certificate. Think of it as the diagnostic step that tells you exactly where you stand and what to work on — whether you are preparing for an official exam or simply need a clear picture of your level for professional or personal reasons.

Reports are delivered bilingually in the assessed language plus your native language — we support 25+ native languages. If your language is not listed, contact us — we may be able to accommodate you.
No preparation needed. This is a natural conversation — not an exam. We assess your real-world speaking ability under normal conditions. Just show up and talk.
