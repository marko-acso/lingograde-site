What You Get

Not just a level. A complete picture.

Most assessments tell you where you are. LingoGrade tells you where you are, why this pattern repeats, and exactly what to do next. Every report is structured into five evidence-based blocks.

Block A

CEFR Assessment

Active and passive CEFR scores with confidence rating. Structural stability analysis. Pattern identification. This is your diagnostic baseline — precise, honest, and actionable.

Included in all packages
Block B

Glitches & Fixes

Your key structural patterns explained in plain language — not just what is happening, but why it keeps happening and exactly how to fix it. With real examples from your own conversation.

Included in all packages
Block C

Personalized Homework

Three targeted exercise types built around your specific weak points: structural writing, precision drills, and guided speaking practice. Ready to start the same day.

Block D

Native Language Translation

Your complete report translated into your mother tongue. Essential for beginners and for anyone who wants to share their results with family, an employer, or a school.

Block E

8-Week Learning Plan

A week-by-week structured plan designed to move you to the next CEFR level. Each phase builds on the previous one with clear milestones and measurable progress.

Think of it as the diagnostic step that tells you exactly where you stand and what to work on — whether you are preparing for an official exam or simply need a clear picture of your level for professional or personal reasons.
