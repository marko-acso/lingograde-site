Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

ACSO Consulting LTD ("we", "us", or "our"), registered in Bulgaria, operates the website www.lingograde.com and provides language learning services under the LingoGrade brand. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you register for our services, enroll in a course, make a payment, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and payment information.

If you sign in using a third-party identity provider (such as Google or Apple), we receive your name, email address, and profile identifier from that provider. We use this information solely to create and manage your LingoGrade account. We do not access your contacts, files, or any other data from these providers beyond what is needed for authentication.

When you make a purchase, payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your full credit card details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

We may also collect technical data automatically when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, through server logs and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process enrollments and payments, deliver language learning services and course materials, communicate with you about your courses and progress, respond to your inquiries and provide support, send you relevant updates about our services, comply with legal obligations, and improve our website and services.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with identity providers (Google, Apple) when you choose to sign in with their accounts, payment processors (Stripe) to complete transactions, service providers who assist in operating our website and delivering our services, and professional advisors and legal authorities when required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All payment transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS technology and processed through PCI-compliant payment infrastructure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Course enrollment and transaction data are retained in accordance with applicable Bulgarian and EU law.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data controller based in the European Union, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, restrict or object to our processing of your data, request data portability, and withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from your account settings at app.lingograde.com. To exercise any other rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Data

LingoGrade offers language assessments for children (under 16). We only collect a child's data when a parent or legal guardian has provided consent. During sessions involving children, a parent or guardian must be present. We collect only the minimum data necessary for the assessment (first name, age range, and session recordings where applicable). We do not create marketing profiles for children, and a parent or guardian may request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies to ensure proper functionality and to analyze site traffic. We use Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC) to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized data such as pages visited, session duration, and approximate location. We have enabled IP anonymization so your full IP address is never stored. You can opt out of Google Analytics by declining cookies in our cookie consent banner or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
