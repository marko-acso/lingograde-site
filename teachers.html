Transparent partnerships

The accusation audit

Every concern you might have about this partnership — addressed before you have to ask.

The Level Concern"What if the assessment places my student lower than I suggested?"

This is the most important question — and we are glad you are asking it. The report never evaluates your teaching. It evaluates the student's current spoken performance under real conditions. If the result is lower than expected, the report frames this as a starting point, not a judgement. And here is the thing teachers tell us: when a student sees their real level in black and white, their motivation to work harder with you goes up, not down. The report literally recommends continued work with their current teacher as the path forward.

The Trust Question"Will my students think I am just trying to earn from this?"

Your students do not see your revenue share. They do not know the details. What they see is a professional assessment tool that their teacher recommended — the same way a doctor recommends a blood test. Nobody suspects the doctor of having a referral deal with the lab. You are recommending a diagnostic because it makes your teaching better. The revenue share is simply how we thank you for that recommendation.

The Money Doubt"Is this just another affiliate programme that pays pennies?"

This is not an affiliate programme — it is a genuine partnership. The revenue share is competitive and designed to reward partners who genuinely believe in the product. There are no tiers to unlock, no minimum thresholds, no expiry dates. Revenue share details are shared upon approval. We would rather have fewer partners who genuinely believe in the product than an army of affiliates pasting links.

The Price Objection"What if my students do not want to pay for an assessment?"

This is where your professional recommendation changes the equation entirely. When a student googles "language assessment" they see a price and hesitate. When their trusted teacher says "I want you to take this assessment so I can build your next three months of lessons around real data" — that is not a transaction - it is a professional recommendation. The students who book through teacher referrals have the highest completion rate and the highest satisfaction scores. Your recommendation is what transforms a service into something worth doing.

The Credibility Check"How do I know LingoGrade assessments meet CEFR standards?"

Every assessment is conducted by a certified language professional using the authoritative CEFR framework - the same standard that governs Goethe-Institut, telc, and Cambridge examinations. Our assessments are calibrated to the European standards with detailed pattern analysis across grammar, vocabulary, fluency, coherence, and pronunciation - all grounded in specific examples from the student's own speech. This is not a chatbot approximation. This is a structured, human-expert diagnostic that produces certified data you can build your entire teaching plan around.

The Risk Question"What does it cost me to join?"

Nothing. There is no signup fee, no monthly charge, no minimum commitment. You sign up, you get your link, you start earning. If you refer zero students, it costs you nothing. If you refer fifty, you earn on all fifty. The risk is entirely ours.
