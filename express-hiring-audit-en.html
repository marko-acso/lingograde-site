Independent. Signed. Defensible.

Methodology: The assessment follows the CEFR Companion Volume (Council of Europe, 2020). Every report is signed with the name and date of the assessor who conducted it. The report contains: the established CEFR level, an assessment against the required target level, linguistic observations across the four skills (listening, speaking, reading, writing) as far as observable in the session, and a clear verdict - fits, fits narrowly, does not fit.

What you do not get: A hiring recommendation. A character assessment. A prediction. We verify a language level at a point in time - everything else remains your decision and your responsibility.

Frequently asked questions

How does this compare on cost?
A Goethe certificate exam typically costs from €250 - and tests academic language competence outside the hiring context. A StepStone ad costs €800-€2,500. A failed hire during probation typically costs €20,000-€50,000 in onboarding, team friction and opportunity. €249.95 is the cheapest insurance in that chain.
What happens if the candidate does not consent?
No assessment, full refund to your card. Automated, no questions asked.
May I use this in the hiring decision?
The audit is an independent CEFR verification of an objectively observable attribute - command of a specific language. It does not replace an entire hiring decision. Compliance with anti-discrimination law remains your responsibility.
What if the 60-minute deadline is missed?
You are entitled to a full refund. We owe you speed - if we do not deliver it, you owe us nothing.
Who conducts the audit?
A named LingoGrade assessor. The name appears on the signed report. Not an anonymized algorithm - a person accountable by name.
