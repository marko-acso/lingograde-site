The cost of a wrong hire

At volume, a language mis-hire is not one bad call. It is a churn problem.

You hire in cohorts of dozens. A written test or a rushed screening call passes people who cannot actually hold the line in English. They reach production, CSAT drops, the client escalates, and the agent washes out in the first month - after you have paid to recruit, onboard, and train them.

Screening spoken English properly, up front, is far cheaper than replacing the agents who should never have passed.
