## NOTES

- All images should be on pure white background for easy background removal
- Maintain consistent proportions: large head (40% of body), stubby wings, round body
- Glasses are round, thin-framed. Cap is always dark navy with gold tassel
- Orange beak and feet are the warm accent that keeps the character approachable
- Mila's purple bow should be consistent size and position (top-right of head)
- Priority order: Batch 1 > Batch 2 > Batch 3 > Batch 4 > Batch 5
