The gap in your training budget

Every year you approve language courses. You never get proof they work.

What arrives at the end is an attendance certificate - "completed." What never arrives: proof that B1 has become B2. You fund an outcome no one measures - and in the next budget cycle you decide on gut feeling again.

It isn't the training that costs too little. It's the training whose result no one measures.

What the Training-Audit is

We don't teach - we measure.

Two independent CEFR measurements, one before the course and one after. Your training sits in between, unchanged. At the end you hold a signed before/after report that documents the level change - to CEFR Companion Volume 2020 (Council of Europe), double-validated.

1

Before

Baseline measurement at course start. A 25-minute conversation, signed report in 60 minutes.

2

Your training runs

The course stays exactly as it is. We don't intervene and we replace nothing.

3

After

Final measurement at course end. You receive the delta report: starting level, final level, documented change.

Methodology & independence

Measured against the standard all of Europe recognises.

Every measurement follows the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR Companion Volume 2020, Council of Europe) and is double-validated. We name telc and Goethe as a benchmark, not as partners - the Training-Audit is the independent measurement beside them, not a certificate replacement and not affiliated with any school.

Data protection: The measured employee is the data subject, gives explicit GDPR consent, and may decline without disadvantage. If consent is not given, the measurement is refunded.
