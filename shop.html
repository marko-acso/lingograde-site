What counts

  • Sticker on a fixed, permanent surface — laptop, wall, window, lamp post, bus stop, campus board
  • Your face — clearly visible, no guessing
  • Marco kissed — plush held close, lips on Marco, his face visible
  • Sticker in frame — sharp, readable, within 30° angle
  • Unlimited locations — every new spot earns commission via QR + EUR 5 selfie bonus

Good to know before you snap

  • Place stickers on a fixed, permanent surface rather than handheld or loose items
  • Make sure your face is clearly visible — no sunglasses or masks
  • Include Marco in the shot with a kiss pose, sticker sharp and in frame
  • Use the original photo — no digital edits, filters, or overlays
  • Each location counts once — find a new spot for the next one
  • Dress appropriately for the setting (swimwear at beach/pool is fine)

Your sticker, your spotlight. You choose the perfect spot — just make sure it’s somewhere appropriate and that the property owner is happy with it.

A quick “mind if I stick this here?” goes a long way. Because we can’t be there when you place it, any questions about a specific location — removal requests, cleaning, or local rules — are between you and the property owner.

To keep the programme fair for everyone, we ask that you follow our placement guidelines. If something looks off — like guideline breaches or suspicious activity — we may need to pause credits while we look into it.

Accusation Audit

You are probably thinking…

Fair questions deserve honest answers.

“What if nobody scans my stickers?” Trust

Every sticker is a permanent billboard. A single sticker on a cafe laptop or campus board gets seen dozens of times a day. You do not need everyone to scan — you need one person curious enough to point their camera. One scan that converts into a booked assessment already earns you more than the sticker cost.

“What if I waste my money?” Money

A single sticker pack is EUR 19.95. One referred assessment booking earns you commission that covers the pack several times over. Even if only 3 out of 35 stickers generate scans, the maths works in your favour. And you still earn EUR 5 selfie credit per verified placement regardless.

“Is this a pyramid scheme?” Credibility

No. There are no levels, no downstream recruits, no upline commissions. You place stickers. People scan your QR code. If they book an assessment, you earn a flat commission. One tier. One relationship: you and LingoGrade. That is it.

“I do not know anyone who needs a language assessment.” Level Concern

You do not need to. The stickers find the people. A QR code on a university campus board, a co-working space, or an airport lounge reaches people you will never meet. Your stickers work while you sleep — 24/7, no introductions needed.

“What if the stickers get removed?” Risk

Some will. That is normal. But a sticker on a laptop lid, a notebook, or inside a window stays for months. Place them where they belong and they stay. If one gets removed, you still earned your selfie credit for that placement. And you still have 34 more.

“EUR 299 for the mega bundle is a lot.” Price

The bundle includes a full assessment, reassessment, homework check, 105 stickers, and Marco — everything individually priced at EUR 384.75. It is there for people who already know they want the complete experience. If you are not sure, start with a single sticker pack or just your free referral link. There is no wrong entry point.

“Do I have to sell or convince people?” Commitment

No selling. No pitching. No chasing. You place a sticker. The QR code does the rest. If someone scans and books, you earn. If they don’t, you still have a fun sticker of Marco on your laptop.

“When do I actually get paid?” Money

Your balance (commission + selfie credit) becomes eligible for cash withdrawal after 10 successful referral assessments. Before that threshold, your balance is spendable on LingoGrade products. Payouts are processed via Stripe — transparent and direct.
