## First Comment (Maker Intro)

*Post this yourself within 60 seconds of launch. 250–350 words. Personal, not promotional.*

---

Hi Product Hunt,

I'm Marko, the founder of LingoGrade.

Three years ago I was tutoring German and noticed something that bothered me: students would study for months, take a Goethe exam, get a certificate — and still have no idea *why* they kept making the same mistakes or what to actually do next. The certificate told them their level. It didn't tell them anything useful.

That's what LingoGrade is built around: the gap between knowing your score and knowing what to do about it.

Here's how it works. You have a 15-minute conversation in your target language with a certified assessor. Within an hour you receive a bilingual PDF report that shows your active and passive CEFR level, the specific patterns causing the most friction, exactly how to fix them, a set of personalized homework exercises, and an 8-week learning plan.

The report is in both your assessed language and your native language — because the last thing a stressed language learner needs is a report they can't fully read.

We assess 12 languages. Reports come back in 25+ native languages. The free bot assessment gives you an instant approximation if you want to try before booking.

A few things I'm genuinely curious about from this community:

1. Is the "professional assessment" framing clear, or does it sound intimidating?
2. What would make you choose LingoGrade over just taking an official exam?
3. Is the free bot assessment the right entry point, or would you want something else first?

Honest feedback carries more weight than upvotes for us right now. Tell me what's unclear, what's missing, what you'd expect that isn't there.

— Marko
